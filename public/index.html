Meta Product Mapping

Meta's ad names (Product + Product Type) don't always match ApparelMagic product names exactly. Map each combination once to a stable internal product family — every future ad with that same Product + Product Type is attributed automatically. Batch No. is metadata only and is never part of the mapping.
