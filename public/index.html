Weekly concept target

How many distinct new creative concepts the team plans each week (default 15). Counts concepts, not products or video exports.

High Stocks

The minimum stock a Platinum or Rocket product needs before it is considered for a creative refresh (High Stocks looks for proven products that have lost momentum, not for stock alone).

Creative Coverage

A product with fewer distinct active Meta ads than this needs new creative. Counts ads that are active, matched (confirmed or auto-matched) and not excluded; paused, archived and unreviewed ads do not count.

Seasonal look-ahead

How far ahead a seasonal lift counts. A season starting within the first window is a strong reason to shoot now, within the second a small one, beyond it none. Seasonality never overrides strong current sales.

Opportunity rules

When a product counts as needing new creative, how long new creative is given to gather data, and how a High Stocks revival is judged. Every value has an allowed range and a default; none is a performance target (those are in Meta Performance).

Priority Overrides

Business reasons that lift (or hold) a product's creative priority whatever its stock, season or coverage: an upcoming drop, a confirmed restock, a promotion, a strategic launch or a manual priority. They only change ranking and its explanation; they never create or change creative.

Performance Benchmarks

Set performance targets for each advertising funnel. These benchmarks power the colour indicators on the Meta Performance page.

  • Each funnel has its own benchmarks. Every ad on Meta Performance is judged only against the benchmarks of its own funnel (TOF, TOM or MOF), even when several funnels are shown together. One funnel's benchmark is never borrowed by another.
  • Leave a benchmark unset and that measure stays neutral. A measure with no benchmark for a funnel is never coloured, and nothing is estimated. Only two targets were pre-approved (MOF CPA and TOF Frequency); everything else is yours to set.
  • Which side is good depends on the measure. CPA, Cost / ATC and CPM: lower is better. Purchase Value, ROAS, Adds to Cart, Outbound CTR and Reach: higher is better. Frequency: lower is better, or give it an acceptable range (lowest and highest). Amount Spent and Purchases are not benchmarked. Purchase Value and Adds to Cart are totals for the period, so give them a reporting window that matches the period length.
  • Green, orange, red. Green meets the target. Orange misses it by up to the tolerance you set (20% unless you change it). Red misses it by more. A value better than the target (for example a CPA well under target) stays green.
  • A benchmark with a reporting window is judged only inside that window. A 3–5-day Frequency target is not applied to a 7-day period: the value shows neutral, and the tooltip says why. Add another window to give the same measure a target for a different period length.
GreenMeets or exceeds target
OrangeWithin the configured tolerance
RedOutside the configured tolerance
Benchmarks are evaluated independently for each funnel. Lower is better for CPA, Cost / ATC, CPM and (usually) Frequency; higher is better for the rest.
